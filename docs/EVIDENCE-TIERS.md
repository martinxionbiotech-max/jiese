# EVIDENCE-TIERS.md — Evidence Tier System

How evidence strength is classified for the knowledge base.

## Tier 1 — Highest
WHO / ICD-11 / NIH / PubMed-indexed systematic reviews, meta-analyses,
clinical guidelines.
Example: WHO ICD-11 inclusion of Compulsive Sexual Behaviour Disorder (6C72)
as an impulse-control disorder.

## Tier 2 — Strong
Peer-reviewed journals, major universities, professional organizations
(e.g., APA, recognized psychological/psychiatric associations).

## Tier 3 — Moderate
High-quality review articles, reputable academic books.

## Tier 4 — Contextual
General health websites — used only for background, never as primary
evidence.

## Not Evidence
Reddit, Baidu Tieba, forums, blogs, and community posts are **never** cited
as scientific evidence. They may appear only as de-identified community
experience, clearly labeled.

## Classification Vocabulary

- **Verified** — multiple Tier 1 sources agree
- **Source-backed** — at least one Tier 1/2 source
- **Reported** — community observation, observational status
- **Estimated** — derived/inferred, not directly measured
- **Unknown** — no evidence identified

## Special Debated Topics

The following are explicitly treated as debated (never one-sided):

1. Whether pornography use constitutes an "addiction" (behavioral addiction
   model vs. impulse-control framework vs. other models)
2. PIED (porn-induced erectile dysfunction) — clinical reports exist; causal
   evidence is limited and contested
3. "Withdrawal" and "flatline" — community terms without established clinical
   definitions; describe them as community terminology
4. "Dopamine detox" concepts — popular claims exceed current evidence
