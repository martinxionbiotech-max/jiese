Failure, or the feeling of having fallen short, is named by some community members as a moment when urges appear, often linked to discouragement or self-criticism.

These reports are personal. A common theme is that members who were able to separate a single setback from their overall progress — and respond to self-criticism with something calmer — described the period after a failure as easier to move through. This is community observation, not evidence.
