Nighttime is a time of day, not a feeling, but community members repeatedly name it as the period when urges feel strongest. Late evening in bed is described especially often, when the day is over and attention has nowhere else to go.

These reports are personal and time-of-day effects have not been scientifically established. Members commonly report that changing nighttime routines — keeping the phone elsewhere, going to bed only when sleepy, having a prepared response — reduces how often late-night urges turn into use.
