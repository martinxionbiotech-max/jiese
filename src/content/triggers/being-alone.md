Being alone, especially at home with a phone or computer nearby, is one of the situations most frequently mentioned in recovery communities. Members often describe privacy as lowering the barriers that would otherwise stop an urge.

These are personal observations, not proof. A recurring theme is that changing the physical situation — leaving the room, moving to a public place, or keeping devices in shared areas — is described as more effective than relying on willpower alone during private time.
