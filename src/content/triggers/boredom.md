Boredom is a feeling of low stimulation or lack of engagement. It is one of the most commonly named triggers in recovery communities, especially for stretches of time that feel open-ended or empty.

Community members often report that having a small, ready list of alternatives — a walk, a chore, a call — makes a difference, because deciding what to do in the middle of a strong urge is described as much harder. These observations come from personal reports and have not been established as evidence.
