Idle time is unstructured free time with nothing planned. Some community members describe urges as appearing most often in these open gaps, when attention has no clear place to go.

These are personal observations. Members commonly report that planning idle time in advance — even loosely — and keeping a short list of quick alternatives reduced how often an empty stretch turned into use.
