Anxiety is a feeling of worry, nervousness, or unease. Some community members describe using pornography as a way to escape or quiet anxious feelings, and others describe feeling more anxious during early days of stopping.

Whether pornography use reduces or worsens anxiety over time is not something community reports can establish. What members do share is that simple calming actions — slow breathing, writing the worry down, reducing caffeine — are sometimes enough to change the moment, and that persistent anxiety is a common reason people say they sought professional help.
