Academic pressure refers to exams, grades, deadlines, and the stress of study. Some community members, particularly students, describe urges as more frequent during high-pressure academic periods.

These are personal reports, and no causal relationship is established. A recurring theme is that changing the study environment — working in public or shared spaces — and scheduling breaks in advance was described as helpful in reducing the automatic turn toward pornography under stress.
