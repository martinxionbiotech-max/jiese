Work pressure refers to deadlines, workload, or stress from a job. Some community members describe urges as showing up more often when work feels overwhelming or out of control.

These are personal reports, not evidence of causation. Members commonly report that small, deliberate breaks and breaking work into smaller steps helped them avoid reacting to stress with pornography, though what works varies widely from person to person.
