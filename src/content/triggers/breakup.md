A breakup is the end of a relationship, and some community members describe the period that follows as one of stronger urges, often connected to sadness, loneliness, or a wish to escape painful feelings.

These are personal reports, not evidence. Members commonly describe keeping basic routines, leaning on supportive people, and being patient with themselves as helpful during this period, and several note that persistent or overwhelming sadness was a reason they sought professional support.
