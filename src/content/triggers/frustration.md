Frustration is a feeling of being blocked, irritated, or unable to make progress. Some community members describe using pornography as a response to feeling frustrated, often quickly and without much deliberation.

These are personal reports. Members commonly describe brief physical movement or stepping away from the frustrating situation as helping the urge pass, but no causal link between frustration and pornography use has been established.
