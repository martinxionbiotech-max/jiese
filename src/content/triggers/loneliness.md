Loneliness is a feeling of disconnection or being alone with one's thoughts. In recovery communities, some members describe using pornography when they feel isolated, as a way to fill quiet or empty-feeling stretches of time.

These are personal reports, not evidence of causation. A recurring theme in what members share is that planned contact — a scheduled call, a shared activity, or telling one trusted person — is described as more helpful than waiting for connection to arrive on its own.
