Dating and dating apps are named by some community members as a context that can lead toward pornography use, whether through the apps themselves or through the feelings of anticipation and nervousness around dating.

These are personal observations. Members commonly report that noticing whether specific apps reliably precede urges — and setting limits on late-night app use — helped them reduce the chain from dating-related feelings to pornography use.
