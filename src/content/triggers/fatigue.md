Fatigue means being tired or low on energy. Some community members describe urges as stronger when they are tired, linking tiredness to weaker restraint and less deliberate decision-making.

These are personal reports. Members commonly report that noticing tiredness early — and resting or avoiding risky situations rather than pushing through — helped them avoid the urge that often followed a long, draining day.
