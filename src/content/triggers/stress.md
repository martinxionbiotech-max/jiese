Stress is a feeling of pressure or strain that people in recovery communities frequently mention when they describe what happened just before an urge. Members often say they turned to pornography almost automatically while feeling overwhelmed, even when they were not otherwise thinking about it.

The connection between stress and urge is reported, not proven. What community reports consistently show is that people who notice stress early and take a small action — stepping away, breathing slowly, telling someone — often describe the urge as passing sooner than when they try to push through it silently. This is a community observation, not a clinical finding.
