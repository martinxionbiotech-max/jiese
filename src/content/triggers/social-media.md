Social media is a set of apps and feeds that many community members name as a place where they encounter content that starts or strengthens an urge. This often happens during long, passive scrolling, particularly late at night.

Members report that the content they see on feeds — even when not explicit — can be enough to begin a chain that leads toward pornography use. These are observations from personal reports; no causal link is established. Practical steps like muting accounts, reducing pre-bed scrolling, and using time limits are commonly mentioned as ways people reduced exposure.
