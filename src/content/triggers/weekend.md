Weekends are days off from work or school, and some community members describe them as a period of stronger urges because structure drops away and free time increases.

These are personal reports. Members commonly report that planning weekend structure in advance — scheduling activities, arranging to be around others, and keeping a regular sleep schedule — reduced how often an unstructured weekend led to use.
