General internet use — aimless browsing, following links, or long unfocused device sessions — is named by some community members as a context in which urges appear, often after drifting toward content they did not intend to seek out.

These are personal observations. Members commonly report that using the internet with a specific purpose, and setting time limits in advance, reduced the drift toward triggering content. No causal relationship is established.
