Phone use is named by many community members as a context for urges, particularly the habit of reaching for the phone automatically in bed or during private time.

These are personal reports. Members commonly report that making the phone less convenient — keeping it out of the bedroom, removing specific apps, or replacing the reaching habit with a different small action — reduced how often a routine reach turned into use.
