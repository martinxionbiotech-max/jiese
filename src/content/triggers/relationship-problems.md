Relationship problems cover conflict, distance, or strain with a partner or someone close. Some community members describe urges or use as happening more often during difficult periods in a relationship.

This is a personal observation, not evidence that relationship problems cause pornography use. A common theme in what members share is that pausing and addressing the underlying conflict directly — rather than turning to pornography — is described as more helpful over time, and that persistent relationship or individual distress is a reason people say they sought support.
