Emotional distress is a broad term for states like sadness, shame, anger, or overwhelm. Some community members describe using pornography as an attempt to escape or numb difficult feelings that are hard to sit with.

These are personal reports. A recurring theme is that letting the feeling be present without acting on it, and talking to someone about it, was described as more helpful than trying to push it away. Persistent or severe distress is a common reason members say they sought professional help.
