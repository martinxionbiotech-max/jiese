Insomnia means difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or sleeping well. Community members sometimes describe lying awake at night as a moment when urges feel stronger and harder to resist.

These reports are personal, and the relationship between sleep and urges is not established. A common theme in what members share is that changing the environment — keeping the phone elsewhere, getting up and doing something calm — is described as more useful than staying in bed while the urge builds.
