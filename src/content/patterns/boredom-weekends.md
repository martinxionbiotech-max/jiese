This pattern describes a recurring observation that weekends and days off appear often in relapse reports, with members linking unstructured time and boredom to stronger urges.

The observation is correlational. It is unclear whether the weekend itself matters, or whether it is the absence of structure and planned activity. Some members describe weekends as easier because of social plans, suggesting free time alone — rather than the calendar day — is what matters. Members who planned their weekends reported fewer urges, but this is community-reported.
