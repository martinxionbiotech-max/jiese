This pattern describes a recurring observation that shame after use is frequently described as leading to more use, creating a cycle members find hard to break.

This is a community observation. Shame is studied in addiction generally, but direct research on shame in problematic pornography use is limited. The direction of the relationship is unclear — shame and use may reinforce each other. Members who were able to reduce shame, often by sharing with someone non-judgmental, describe the cycle as easier to interrupt, but this is reported, not demonstrated.
