This pattern describes a recurring observation that the first days and weeks after stopping are often described as the period of strongest urges and highest relapse risk.

This is a community observation, not an established timeline. The concept of withdrawal and early-abstinence difficulty is discussed in PPU literature but remains debated. Some members report the opposite pattern, suggesting wide individual variation. No firm timeline should be assumed.
