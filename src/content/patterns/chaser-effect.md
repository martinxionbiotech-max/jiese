This pattern describes a community-described effect in which a short period of stronger urges follows any sexual activity, including pornography use, in the following hours or days.

This is not established scientifically. Some members report no such effect, and others report the opposite — reduced interest immediately after use. The concept is widely used in recovery communities but should be treated as a community description, not a research finding.
