This pattern describes a recurring observation that exposure to suggestive or sexualized content on social media appears repeatedly in reports as a step that precedes pornography use.

This is correlational. Many people are exposed to the same content without progressing to problematic use, so exposure alone is not sufficient. The content may act as a cue that activates an urge in people who are already sensitive to it. No direct research on this specific path has been identified.
