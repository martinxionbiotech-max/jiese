This pattern describes a recurring observation that being alone at home with a device is the setting where urges are described as hardest to resist.

This is correlational. It is unclear whether being alone matters or whether it is the combination of privacy, habit, and device access. Some members report that time alone at home was fine when it was structured or when devices were kept out of reach, suggesting the specific conditions matter more than solitude itself.
