This pattern describes a recurring observation: many relapse reports mention late-night phone use, often scrolling in bed that gradually drifts toward pornography use. It is one of the most commonly described situations across recovery community reports.

The observation is correlational. It is not clear whether the phone causes the relapse or whether late night simply combines tiredness, privacy, and habit in a way that makes relapse more likely. Members who changed their nighttime phone habits often describe fewer late-night relapses, but this is community-reported, not demonstrated.
