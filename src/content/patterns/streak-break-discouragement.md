This pattern describes a recurring observation that breaking a long streak is frequently followed by discouragement, and sometimes by reduced motivation to continue.

This is correlational. It is unclear whether streak length itself drives the discouragement, or whether the meaning the person attaches to the streak matters more. Some members report a quick restart after a slip when they framed it as a single event rather than losing everything. This is community observation, not established evidence.
