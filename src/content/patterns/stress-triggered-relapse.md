This pattern describes a recurring observation that relapses are often reported during periods of high stress, with members describing pornography use as an almost automatic response to feeling overwhelmed.

This is a correlational observation. Stress is widely studied in relation to compulsive behaviors generally, but direct research linking stress specifically to PPU relapse is limited. It is also not universal: some members describe relapses during calm, unstructured periods instead. Stress may precede relapse without being its sole cause.
