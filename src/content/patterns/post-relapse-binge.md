This pattern describes a recurring observation that a single relapse is often followed by a period of heavier use, which members describe as a binge before a renewed attempt to stop.

This is correlational. The related concept in addiction research — sometimes called the abstinence violation effect — holds that the emotional response to a slip can drive continued use, but this concept is debated and has not been directly established for PPU. Some members report that treating a slip without self-blame prevented a binge, suggesting the emotional response to the slip matters.
