Phone restrictions are self-imposed limits on phone use. Community members often describe keeping the phone out of the bedroom, leaving it in another room, or removing specific apps as ways of reducing how easily a routine reach turns into use.

This is a community-reported strategy. Because phone use is so often tied to nighttime and alone-time urges, changing where and when the phone is available is one of the most frequently mentioned practical changes. Direct research on this as a PPU strategy is limited.
