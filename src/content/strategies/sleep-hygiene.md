Sleep hygiene means the habits that support consistent, restful sleep. Community members often describe improving their sleep — keeping phones out of the bedroom, keeping a regular schedule, winding down deliberately — as reducing the late-night windows when urges felt strongest.

This is a community-reported strategy. Good sleep habits have broad general evidence for health and mood, but direct study of sleep hygiene as a strategy for problematic pornography use is limited. Its value is indirect: it reduces tiredness and the unstructured, screen-heavy hours where many people describe urges.
