Journaling is writing about urges, feelings, and progress. Community members often describe it as a way to slow down an automatic reaction — writing what happened and what preceded it instead of immediately using — and to spot patterns over time.

This is a community-reported strategy. Direct research on journaling for problematic pornography use is limited. Members note that journaling works best when done consistently and used to inform action, rather than as a record alone.
