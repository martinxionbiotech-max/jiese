Social accountability means telling someone you trust and checking in with them regularly. Community members often describe secrecy as part of the problem, and sharing as a way to reduce it and gain support.

This is a community-reported strategy. Social support has broader evidence for helping behavior change, but direct research on accountability specifically for problematic pornography use is limited. Members note that it depends on finding someone trustworthy and non-judgmental, which is not available to everyone.
