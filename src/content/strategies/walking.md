Walking is one of the simplest strategies people report: when an urge appears, they leave the room and take a short walk. The change of scene and the movement are described as helping the urge pass.

This is a community-reported strategy, not an evidence-based treatment. Members commonly note that walking works best as a way to interrupt the moment, especially when alone, bored, or stressed, and that it is easiest when it requires no special equipment or planning.
