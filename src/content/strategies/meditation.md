Meditation is a practice of returning attention to the present, often using the breath. Some community members describe it as helping them observe an urge as a passing feeling rather than something they must immediately act on.

This is a community-reported strategy. Mindfulness and meditation have broader research support for attention and emotional regulation, but direct study of meditation specifically for problematic pornography use is limited. Members often note that it works best when practiced regularly, not only in moments of crisis.
