Exercise is physical activity used deliberately. In recovery communities, it is one of the most commonly mentioned ways of responding to an urge: people describe going for a walk or run, or working out, as a way to change the moment and let the urge pass.

Exercise is not presented here as a cure. What members report is that moving the body helped them step out of an urge, particularly when the urge was tied to stress, boredom, or frustration. Research supports exercise generally for mood and stress, but direct study of exercise as a treatment for problematic pornography use is limited.
