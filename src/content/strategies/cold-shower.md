A cold shower is a short, deliberately uncomfortable physical reset. Some community members describe taking a brief cold shower, or splashing cold water on their face, as a way to interrupt a strong urge by changing physical state suddenly.

This is a community-reported strategy with essentially no direct research for problematic pornography use. Its effect is anecdotal and may simply be a strong form of distraction. Members describe it as most useful in the moment rather than as a long-term solution.
