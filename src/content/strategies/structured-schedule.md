A structured schedule means planning the day in advance to reduce the open, unstructured time where urges tend to appear. Community members often describe evenings and weekends — when structure drops away — as high-risk, and planning activities for those windows as helpful.

This is a community-reported strategy. Direct research is limited. Members note that scheduling works best when it is realistic and includes rest, since over-scheduling can add stress and backfire.
