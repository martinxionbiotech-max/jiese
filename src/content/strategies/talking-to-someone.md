Talking to someone means reaching out to another person — a friend, family member, or support group — when an urge appears or when difficult feelings build. Community members often describe isolation and secrecy as part of what makes urges harder to resist, and contact as a way to break that.

This is a community-reported strategy. Social support has broader evidence, but direct research on this specific approach for problematic pornography use is limited. It depends on having someone available and trustworthy, which is not the case for everyone.
