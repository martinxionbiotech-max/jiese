Reducing social media means cutting down or curating social media use to reduce exposure to content that precedes urges. Community members often describe passive scrolling — especially late at night — as a path that leads toward pornography use.

This is a community-reported strategy. Direct research is limited. Members note that the goal is not necessarily to remove social media entirely, since it can also be a source of support, but to reduce the specific exposure that reliably precedes urges.
