Environment change means deliberately altering the physical or social setting to reduce the conditions where urges appear. Community members describe moving devices to shared spaces, spending more time in public, or changing how a room is used.

This is a community-reported strategy. Direct research is limited. Members note that environment change works by reducing opportunity and convenience rather than by removing the urge, and that some environments — such as living alone — are difficult to change.
