Website blockers are software or settings that prevent access to certain sites. Community members often describe them as adding a barrier between an urge and the act of using, giving a moment of delay in which the urge can pass.

This is a community-reported strategy, and direct research on blockers for problematic pornography use is limited and mixed. Members commonly note that blockers work best as one part of a broader approach, since they can be bypassed and do not address the urge itself.
