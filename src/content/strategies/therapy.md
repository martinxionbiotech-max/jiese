Therapy means working with a qualified mental health professional. Among the strategies listed on this platform, it has the strongest claim to evidence, since psychotherapy — including cognitive behavioral approaches — has research support for treating compulsive sexual behaviors, and direct evidence specific to problematic pornography use continues to develop.

Therapy is not a quick fix and does not work the same way for everyone. Access, cost, and finding a non-judgmental professional are real barriers. Community members who have used therapy often describe it as most helpful for understanding the feelings and situations behind the behavior, rather than just suppressing urges.
