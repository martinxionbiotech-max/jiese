"Flatline" is a term used in recovery communities to describe a reported period of low sexual desire or interest, sometimes with reduced morning erections or a sense of sexual "numbness," during an attempt to stop using pornography.

It is important to be clear that the flatline is not a medically established phenomenon. It is a community description, and no scientific evidence documents a distinct flatline phase. Some members report it; others never experience it, and some report the opposite.

Because the term carries no clinical meaning, this platform describes it only as what it is: a community-reported experience that some people find reassuring and others find worrying. Persistent or concerning changes in sexual function are best discussed with a professional.
