The 90-day challenge is a community idea of abstaining from pornography for 90 days. It originated in recovery communities and is used widely as a shared motivational goal.

It is not a treatment, and there is no research establishing that 90 days specifically produces any particular outcome. Some members find a fixed goal motivating; others find arbitrary timelines unhelpful or even discouraging when they do not reach them.

The honest framing is that a 90-day goal can be a useful structure, but the number itself has no demonstrated significance. Progress does not depend on a specific streak.
