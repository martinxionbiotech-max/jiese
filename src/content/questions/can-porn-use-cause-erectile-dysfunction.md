Whether pornography use causes erectile dysfunction is a common question, and the honest answer is that it is not settled.

Evidence on a causal link is mixed, and the claim is debated. Erectile dysfunction has many possible causes, including anxiety, stress, sleep, and general health, so attributing it to pornography alone is often premature. Some men do report that erectile function improved after they stopped using pornography, and this platform reports those experiences without treating them as proof.

Persistent erectile dysfunction is best evaluated by a doctor rather than self-diagnosed.
