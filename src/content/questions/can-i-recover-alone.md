People often ask whether they can recover on their own. The honest answer is that recovery paths vary widely.

Some community members report making progress largely alone, using self-directed strategies such as changing their environment, planning their time, and tracking triggers. Others say that telling someone they trust, joining a group, or seeking professional help was the turning point, particularly when secrecy felt like part of the problem.

There is no research establishing who is likely to succeed alone versus with support. A reasonable approach is to start with self-directed strategies, notice what is and is not working, and stay open to support if progress stalls.
