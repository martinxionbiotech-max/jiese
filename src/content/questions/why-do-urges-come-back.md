Urges returning is one of the most common and most discouraging experiences people report. The key point is that urges are expected, not a sign that recovery has failed.

Urges are typically tied to triggers — situations, feelings, or cues such as stress, boredom, being alone, or encountering certain content. Because these triggers reappear in daily life, urges reappear too. Many community members describe urges as passing within minutes if not acted on.

Research on urges specifically in problematic pornography use is limited, though cue-triggered craving is studied more generally in compulsive behaviors. Why urges fade for some people and persist for others is not well understood.
