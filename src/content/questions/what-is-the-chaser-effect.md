The "chaser effect" is a community term for a reported period of stronger urges in the hours or days following sexual activity, including pornography use.

It is not scientifically established. Some members describe a short period of heightened urges after use, while others report no such effect, and some report the opposite — reduced interest immediately after use. No direct research confirms a reliable chaser period.

The practical value of the idea, for those who experience it, is as a reminder to plan extra structure and prepare for urges in the period after any use or sexual activity, including after a slip.
