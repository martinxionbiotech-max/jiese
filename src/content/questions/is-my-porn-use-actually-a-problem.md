Whether your pornography use is "actually a problem" is a question only you can answer, but there are useful ways to think about it.

The core test is not the amount of time spent, but whether use feels out of control, causes distress, or creates problems in your life. Repeated failed attempts to cut back are a common early sign people describe. This is consistent with how problematic use and CSBD are framed clinically — by distress and impaired control, not by a specific frequency.

There is no universal threshold, and where the line lies is debated. A professional can help you assess your use without judgment if you are unsure.
