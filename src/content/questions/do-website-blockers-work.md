Website blockers are software or settings that restrict access to pornography and other triggering sites. Some community members report that blockers helped by creating a delay — a few seconds of friction during which an urge could pass.

Others report that blockers did not help because they simply disabled them or found ways around them. Direct research on blockers for problematic pornography use is limited and mixed.

The reasonable summary is that blockers can add friction but are not a reliable solution on their own. They are best understood as one part of a broader approach that also changes environment, routines, and support.
