What counts as a relapse is less obvious than it sounds, and there is no universal definition.

For most people, a relapse means returning to pornography use after intending to stop. But members differ on the details: some count any use, while others distinguish a single slip from a return to the old pattern. There is no standardized definition in the research.

A practical approach is to decide in advance what counts as a relapse for you, and to focus on the overall pattern rather than any single event. Be cautious about rules so strict that any slip feels catastrophic, since that can itself feed the shame spiral.
