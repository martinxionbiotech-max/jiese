Nighttime is one of the most commonly reported high-risk periods, particularly late evening in bed with a phone nearby.

The strategies members most often describe are environmental rather than willpower-based: keeping devices out of the bedroom, leaving the room when an urge appears, and building a consistent wind-down routine. Members also report that deciding a response in advance works better than deciding in the middle of a strong urge.

These are community observations; no direct research establishes time-of-day effects on urges in problematic pornography use.
