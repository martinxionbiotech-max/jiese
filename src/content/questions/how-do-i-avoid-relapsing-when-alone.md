Being alone with a device is one of the most frequently described high-risk situations in recovery communities.

The strategies members most often report are environmental rather than willpower-based: leaving the room, moving to a public space, keeping devices in shared areas, and planning alone time in advance. Members consistently describe changing the environment as more effective than trying to resist an urge through willpower alone.

These techniques are community-reported, not established by direct research, but they are among the most commonly repeated practical suggestions in recovery discussions.
