The "reboot" movement is a loose online community of people attempting to stop or reduce pornography use, often framed around the idea of "rebooting" the brain from the effects of pornography.

This platform distinguishes the peer support — which many members found genuinely helpful — from the brain-based claims, which are not supported by established science. The "reboot" framing is a community idea, not a scientific finding.

The reasonable approach is to take the peer support while being skeptical of unsupported claims, and to evaluate any specific claim against the evidence layer rather than accepting it because a community repeats it.
