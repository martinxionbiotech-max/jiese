The clearest way pornography can affect sleep is through timing: late-night use delays bedtime, and members often describe this pattern of using porn late at night and feeling tired the next day.

Whether pornography itself has any specific effect on sleep quality, beyond general late-night screen use, is not established. Screen use and late-night stimulation can interfere with sleep generally, but direct research on pornography and sleep is limited.

The practical advice is the same as for late-night urges generally: keep devices out of the bedroom and build a wind-down routine that does not involve screens.
