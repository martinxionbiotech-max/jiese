Some people hope that quitting pornography will fix a troubled relationship, and sometimes secrecy around porn use is indeed a source of strain.

But this platform makes no promise that quitting repairs relationships. Relationship problems usually involve multiple factors, and removing one source of strain does not resolve underlying issues of communication and trust. Some members report that quitting helped reduce secrecy and improve trust; others found that deeper problems remained.

A more realistic framing is that quitting can be one step, but relationship problems are usually addressed directly, sometimes with professional support.
