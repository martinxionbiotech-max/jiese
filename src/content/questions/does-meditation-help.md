Meditation is a practice of returning attention to the present, often through the breath. Some community members report that it helps them observe an urge as a passing feeling rather than something they must immediately act on.

Meditation and mindfulness have broader research support for attention and emotional regulation, but direct study of meditation specifically for problematic pornography use is limited. Members often note that it works best when practiced regularly, not pulled out only in moments of crisis.

Treating meditation as one tool among several, and building a short daily habit, is the approach most consistent with both the community reports and the limited evidence.
