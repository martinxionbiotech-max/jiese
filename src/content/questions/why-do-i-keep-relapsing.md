Relapse means returning to the behavior after a period of stopping or reducing it. It is one of the most discussed topics in recovery communities, and one of the most distressing for people who experience it.

The key point this platform makes is that relapse is common and does not mean the attempt failed. Community members most often describe relapses as tied to specific situations — stress, boredom, being alone, or late at night — rather than to a lack of willpower. Understanding your own patterns is described as more useful than self-blame.

Research on relapse specifically in problematic pornography use is limited, though relapse is well-documented in compulsive behaviors generally. There is no reliable way to predict relapse or a fixed timeline for recovery.
