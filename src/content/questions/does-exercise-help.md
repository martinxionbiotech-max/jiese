Exercise is one of the most commonly reported strategies in recovery communities. Members describe walking, running, or working out as a way to change the moment and let an urge pass, particularly when it is tied to stress, boredom, or frustration.

Exercise has broad evidence for improving mood and managing stress, which is a real and well-supported benefit. Direct research on exercise specifically for problematic pornography use is limited, however, so this platform reports it as community-reported rather than as a proven treatment.

Using movement as an immediate response, and building a regular routine, is the approach most consistent with what members report.
