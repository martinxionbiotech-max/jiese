Recovery is rarely a straight line, and this is one of the clearest messages in recovery communities.

Members overwhelmingly describe recovery as non-linear: progress, then setbacks, then plateaus, then more progress. Several attempts before longer periods of control are the norm in what people report. The practical implication is to expect setbacks, treat them as information, and track the overall trend over weeks rather than judging any single day.

Relapse and non-linear change are well-documented in compulsive behaviors generally, even if direct research on problematic pornography use specifically is limited.
