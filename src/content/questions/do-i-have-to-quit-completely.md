People often assume recovery requires complete, permanent abstinence, but that is not necessarily true.

The goal this platform emphasizes is regaining a sense of control and reducing the distress or problems connected to pornography use. For some people that means complete abstinence; for others it means cutting back to a level that no longer feels out of control. Both are valid goals, and no research establishes that abstinence is necessary or superior for everyone.

A practical approach is to define your own goal, start with something realistic, and revisit it as you learn what works for you.
