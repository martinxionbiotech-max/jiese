Some people report feeling low or flat after stopping pornography use, which can be discouraging.

A possible explanation is that pornography was serving as a way to escape difficult feelings, and stopping removes that escape. Alternatively, low mood that was already present may become more noticeable. No established research documents a specific depression syndrome from quitting.

Keeping basic routines, staying in contact with supportive people, and noticing whether the low mood is new are practical starting points. Persistent or severe low mood — especially with thoughts of self-harm — is a reason to seek professional help promptly.
