Problematic pornography use (PPU) is a term for pornography use that feels out of control, causes distress, or creates problems in a person's life. It is a behavioral description, not a formal diagnosis.

Researchers use PPU to describe a pattern that includes repeated attempts to cut back that do not stick, use that continues despite wanting to stop, and negative consequences or feelings afterward. The term deliberately avoids the "addiction" label, which remains debated.

This platform uses PPU as its default framing, describing the problem in behavioral terms rather than insisting on a particular diagnostic model.
