Some people report feeling more anxious after they stop using pornography, and this can be confusing and discouraging.

A possible explanation is that pornography was serving as a way to avoid or escape difficult feelings, and stopping removes that escape, allowing the feelings to surface. Alternatively, anxiety that was already present may simply become more noticeable. This platform reports the experience without claiming a causal link, since no established research documents a specific anxiety syndrome from quitting.

Keeping basic routines, using calming practices, and noticing whether the anxiety is new are practical starting points. Persistent or severe anxiety is worth discussing with a professional.
