Some men report that erectile function or interest in partnered sex improved after they stopped or reduced pornography use, and this is a common reason people give for quitting.

The important caveat is that these are self-reports. Evidence on the link between pornography use and erectile function is mixed, and the popular concept of "porn-induced erectile dysfunction" is debated and not firmly established in mainstream medicine. Erectile difficulties have many possible causes, including anxiety, sleep, stress, and general health.

This platform therefore reports what people say — some improved, some did not — without claiming that quitting causes improvement in sexual function.
