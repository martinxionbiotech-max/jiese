Masturbation and pornography use are different things, even though they often happen together. This platform focuses on problematic pornography use, not on masturbation itself.

There is no established scientific basis for treating masturbation itself as harmful. The research focus is on pornography use that feels out of control and causes distress or problems.

Members hold a wide range of personal views on masturbation, and some treat it as a separate question from the pornography use they are trying to stop. A useful first step is to clarify for yourself what you are actually trying to change, and to notice whether masturbation reliably leads you back to pornography.
