Sexual thoughts are a normal part of being human, and having them is not a failure or a relapse.

The problem is not the thought itself but the chain from thought to pornography use. Members frequently report that trying to suppress sexual thoughts backfires — the effort to push a thought away tends to make it stronger — while simply noticing it and letting it pass, or shifting attention to something concrete, is more useful.

Thought suppression being counterproductive is well established in general psychology, though direct research on this specific question in problematic pornography use is limited.
