The claim that quitting pornography raises testosterone circulates widely in recovery communities, but it is not supported by established science.

Available research does not support a link between pornography use and testosterone levels, and there is no established mechanism for such a link. The idea functions more as a motivational belief than a verified finding.

This platform is direct about this: testosterone claims should not be used as the reason to quit. People quit for real, personal reasons — control, reduced distress, relationships — and those reasons are enough without unsupported hormonal claims.
