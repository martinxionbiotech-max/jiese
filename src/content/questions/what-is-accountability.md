Accountability means telling someone you trust and checking in with them regularly, in order to reduce the secrecy that often surrounds problematic pornography use.

Members frequently describe accountability — with a friend, partner, or group — as a turning point, because it reduces both secrecy and shame. Social support has broader evidence for helping behavior change, though direct research on accountability specifically for problematic pornography use is limited.

The practical forms are simple: tell one trusted person, set up regular check-ins, and share both slips and successes. A therapist can provide confidential accountability if you have no one you feel safe telling.
