Many community members report that urges feel strongest in the first days and weeks after stopping, and this is one of the most common descriptions in recovery reports.

It is important to note that this is a common experience, not an established or universal pattern. Some people report the opposite, and individual experience varies widely. Early-abstinence difficulty is discussed in the literature but is not firmly established.

The practical implication is to prepare for a harder early period — reducing exposure to triggers, planning responses in advance, and using support — without treating strong early urges as a sign that something is wrong.
