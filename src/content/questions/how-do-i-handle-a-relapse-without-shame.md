Handling a relapse without shame is one of the hardest and most important skills people describe learning.

Members who manage this well describe a few specific moves: treating the relapse as information about triggers rather than a verdict on character, noticing self-critical thoughts and responding to them with something calmer, and telling someone rather than keeping it secret. Secrecy and shame reinforce each other.

Shame is studied in addiction generally, but direct research on shame in problematic pornography use is limited. The community consensus, such as it is, is that reducing shame is what allows a quick restart.
