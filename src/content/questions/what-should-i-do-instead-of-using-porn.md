The question of what to do instead of using pornography has a consistent community answer: whatever changes the moment.

Members emphasize two things. First, the replacement should change your physical situation — leaving the room, moving, doing something hands-on, contacting someone — rather than just trying to think your way out. Second, alternatives work better when prepared in advance, because deciding in the middle of a strong urge is hard.

A short, ready list of quick, always-available alternatives — movement, hands-on tasks, and contact with others — is the most commonly described approach.
