Some members report that frequent pornography use affected their concentration, often through two indirect routes: preoccupation with use during the day, or fatigue from late-night use.

No established research demonstrates that pornography use causes lasting concentration problems, and it is unclear whether any difficulties are caused by the use itself or by related factors such as sleep loss. The practical first step is to address sleep and stress, which are common contributors to poor focus, and to track when concentration problems actually occur.
