Streak tracking — counting days without use — is popular in recovery communities. It can be genuinely motivating for some people, giving a visible sense of progress.

But it can also backfire. Members frequently report that breaking a long streak caused discouragement, and sometimes made restarting harder because they felt they had "lost everything." The same number that motivated them became a source of shame.

There is no research establishing whether streak tracking helps or harms recovery. The reasonable guidance is to track if it motivates you, stop if it becomes punishing, and track what actually helps, not just days.
