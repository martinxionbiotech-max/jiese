Relapsing after a long streak is one of the most discouraging experiences people report, and it is common.

Members describe a few recurring reasons: a trigger reappearing after being absent, a change in routine that removed the structure that was helping, a stressful event, or a moment of overconfidence in which the strategies that got them there were relaxed. No research establishes a specific explanation.

The practical takeaways are to expect triggers to still appear, to keep using the strategies that worked rather than relaxing them, and to treat a long-streak relapse as a single event rather than losing everything.
