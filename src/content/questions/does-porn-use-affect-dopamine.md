Dopamine is involved in the brain's reward system, and pornography use, like any rewarding or pleasurable activity, involves dopamine. In that limited sense, it is true that pornography use "involves dopamine."

The popular claims that go further — that pornography "depletes," "damages," or "fries" dopamine — are oversimplifications that are not supported by established science. There is no established evidence that pornography use causes lasting dopamine depletion or brain damage.

This platform treats dopamine explanations with caution, and points to the evidence layer for a more careful account. Practical recovery work focuses on triggers, habits, and strategies rather than brain-chemistry claims.
