Compulsive Sexual Behaviour Disorder (CSBD) is a diagnosis in the World Health Organization's ICD-11, classified as an impulse-control disorder. It is characterized by a persistent pattern of failure to control intense sexual impulses, resulting in significant distress or impairment.

CSBD is not the same as "porn addiction," which is a popular term rather than a formal diagnosis. The relationship between CSBD and problematic pornography use is an active area of research, and how well the diagnosis maps onto pornography use specifically is debated.
