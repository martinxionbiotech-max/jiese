People want a timeline for when they will feel better, but the honest answer is that no established timeline exists.

Community members report widely varying experiences: some describe improvement within weeks, others over months, and some report no dramatic change but a growing sense of control. Many say the more reliable change was a feeling of being back in control rather than a sudden sense of being "fixed."

The practical approach is to let go of a fixed deadline, notice small signs of progress, and focus on control and reduced distress rather than a single moment of being cured.
