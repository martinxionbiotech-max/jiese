Telling someone you trust is one of the most commonly recommended steps in recovery communities, and also one of the most difficult.

Members who have shared describe a few practical points: choose someone who is unlikely to judge, start with what you are struggling with rather than every detail, and say what kind of support you are asking for. The first conversation does not need to cover everything.

There is no research establishing a specific method, but social support is generally associated with better outcomes in behavior change. If you have no one you feel safe telling, a therapist or a confidential support group is an alternative.
